# Privacy Policy — On Cue Teleprompter

Last updated: 22 August 2026

On Cue does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to its developer. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

## Your scripts

Scripts you type, or open from Files or iCloud Drive, stay on your device. If you open a file, edits save back to that same file automatically as you type — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and nothing leaves your device to make that happen.

## Your settings

Text size, alignment, margins, pace and the other options in Prompter settings are stored on your device using iOS's standard settings storage, so they carry over the next time you open the app. They are never transmitted anywhere.

## Your recordings

Takes recorded with the front camera are saved to your own Photos library and nowhere else. On Cue does not upload, copy or transmit them.

## Speech recognition

On Cue uses Apple's Speech framework to follow your place in the script. It asks for on-device recognition, which keeps the audio on your device.

If your device has not downloaded the offline speech model — it arrives when you enable Dictation in Settings → General → Keyboard, or set up Siri — iOS falls back to Apple's speech service, and audio is sent to Apple to be transcribed. This is the same processing that Dictation performs, and it is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). On Cue receives only the resulting text and stores none of it.

## Permissions

- **Microphone** — to hear you speak so the script can follow along.
- **Speech recognition** — to turn that audio into words to match against your script.
- **Camera** — to record your take, only while you are in the prompter.
- **Photos (add only)** — to save a finished recording. On Cue cannot read your existing photos.

You can withdraw any of these at any time in Settings, and the app will tell you what stops working.

## Children

On Cue collects no data, from anyone, including children.
